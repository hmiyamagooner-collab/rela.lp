Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

This English version of the Privacy Policy applies to users who access RELA in English, including users in the United States. Users in Japan are governed by the Japanese プライバシーポリシー. Section 13 contains additional disclosures for California residents.

GOONER CO., LTD. (株式会社Gooner) (“GOONER,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates RELA (the “Service”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. GOONER is based in Japan; your information is processed in Japan and by service providers in the United States and other countries.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and other registration and authentication data (an anonymous account may be created first and linked to an email later).
  • Profile information you enter: gender, date of birth, blood type, personality-test answers, and other profile details.
  • Information about other people you register: nickname, age, relationship notes, and similar details about the people you analyze.
  • Uploads: chat screenshots, photos of faces and palms, and other images or text you upload.
  • Results and usage: AI analysis results, generated content, feature usage and interaction history, and support correspondence.
  • Technical information: IP address, cookies and local storage identifiers, device, OS and browser information, access logs, and error reports.
  • Payment information: name, email, billing details, purchase and subscription status. Card numbers are collected and stored by our payment processors (Stripe, Google Play); we do not store them.

2. How We Use Information

  • To register, authenticate, provide, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • To generate and display AI analyses, suggested replies, images, reports, and other results you request;
  • To manage paid plans, free trials, Coins, payments, and contracts;
  • To send important notices, such as changes to terms and service outages;
  • To respond to inquiries and requests;
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, violations of our Terms, and security threats;
  • To create de-identified statistics, improve quality, and analyze usage; and
  • To comply with legal obligations and to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

3. Service Providers and AI Processing

  • We share information with service providers only as needed to operate the Service. Our main providers are Supabase (authentication and data storage), OpenAI and Anthropic (AI analysis), Stripe (web payments), Google (Android app distribution and Google Play billing), and RevenueCat (subscription status management).
  • Information sent to AI providers consists of all or part of the text and images you enter or upload, together with the instructions needed to perform the analysis. We use the providers' API and business services under contracts and settings under which data we send is not used to train their foundation models.
  • Service providers may process information outside your country. We select and supervise providers, restrict access, and confirm their security practices as required by applicable law.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose personal information only:

  • To the service providers described in Section 3, who may use it only to provide services to us;
  • When you direct us to, or with your consent;
  • To comply with law, legal process, or government requests, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of you, us, or others;
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case we will notify you of any change in the handling of your information.

5. Information About Other People

  • Before entering or uploading information about another person, obtain that person's consent. Mask names, addresses, phone numbers, employers, and other directly identifying details where possible, and do not enter sensitive information (see our Terms of Service).
  • We may delete information that may violate the law or infringe someone's rights. If a person asks us to delete information about them, we will verify the request and respond in accordance with applicable law.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies, browser local storage, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, protect the Service, troubleshoot issues, remember settings such as your language, and understand how the Service is used. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can restrict cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the Service may then stop working.

7. Data Retention

  • We keep personal information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described above and as required by law.
  • After you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except for information we must retain for legal compliance or dispute resolution.
  • Data in backups is deleted according to our backup cycle, generally within 90 days after account deletion.
  • Payment and transaction records are kept for the periods required by law. Our service providers retain data according to our contracts and settings with them.

8. Security

  • We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse, and unauthorized access or disclosure, including assigning responsibility, restricting access, encrypting communications, managing credentials, reviewing logs, supervising providers, and training staff.
  • If a data breach occurs, we will investigate, contain it, prevent recurrence, and notify affected users and authorities as required by law.

9. International Transfers

We are located in Japan, and information is stored and processed in Japan and by providers in the United States and other countries whose data-protection laws may differ from those of your home jurisdiction. We protect transferred data through contracts and the safeguards described in this Policy.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Users aged 13 to 17 may use the Service only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

11. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access, correction, and deletion: you can view and edit your profile and registered people in the Service, and delete your account from Settings, which deletes your data as described in Section 7.
  • Subject to applicable law, you may also request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a portable copy of your personal information, or object to or restrict certain processing, by contacting us at info@gooner-info.co.jp. We will verify your identity (for example, by confirming control of your account email) and respond within the time required by law, generally by email. If we cannot fulfill a request, we will explain why.
  • Marketing: we do not send marketing email. Service notices may still be sent as needed to operate your account.

12. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Because we do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, browser “Do Not Track” and Global Privacy Control signals do not change how we process your information.
